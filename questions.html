Old-syllabus MEI questions

Questions from the old MEI A Level papers (module C3, 2011 to 2018), before the current specification. Do each one on paper, then check it against the mark scheme.

Old syllabus. These papers were written for the old course, so the style is more structured than your exam will be and a few parts may use slightly different methods or notation. They are great practice for the skills, but use the current MEI papers to see what your real exam looks like.

Pick a skill, then work through it one question at a time. Each skill starts with the questions worth the fewest marks. Long questions are the big 15 to 18 mark questions from the end of the paper, and some parts use other topics too.
